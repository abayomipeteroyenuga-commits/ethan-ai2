# Ethan Office v17.2

- Expanded the active slide surface to fill the entire available presentation editing stage.
- Removed stage padding, slide shadows, outer borders, and rounded corners in the editor so the slide reads as one continuous presentation canvas.
- Preserved the ribbon, slide thumbnails, notes pane, themes, transitions, and presentation tools.
- Mobile/tablet presentation stage also uses the full available canvas.
