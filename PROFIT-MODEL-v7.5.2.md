# ETHAN AI Profit-Protected Premium Model — v7.5.2
Reviewed 8 September 2026.

## Current OpenAI rates used
GPT-5.6 Luna: $0.20 / 1M input tokens; $1.20 / 1M output tokens.
Web Search: $10 / 1,000 web runs ($0.01/run), plus search-content tokens at model rates.

## Internal planning budget
Baseline example: 1 web run + 5,000 input tokens + 1,000 output tokens ≈ $0.0122/search.
ETHAN AI does NOT budget at the bare minimum. It reserves $0.015 per Premium AI search to absorb variation.

## Plans and maximum AI budget
Free — $0: Free Public Search; no OpenAI calls.
Plus — $2.99: 40 Premium AI searches; AI budget $0.60; gross contribution $2.39; gross contribution margin ~79.9%.
Pro — $6.99: 150 Premium AI searches; AI budget $2.25; gross contribution $4.74; gross contribution margin ~67.8%.
Education/Business — $14.99: 350 Premium AI searches; AI budget $5.25; gross contribution $9.74; gross contribution margin ~65.0%.

These are contribution margins after the reserved OpenAI search budget only. Payment fees, taxes, hosting, support, refunds and other operating costs still come out of the remainder.

## Protection rules
- Free users never call OpenAI.
- Paid entitlement is verified server-side.
- Monthly AI quota is server-enforced.
- When quota is exhausted, the user automatically retains Free Public Search.
- Premium AI is disabled/falls back to Free Search when OpenAI is not configured.
- Review actual OpenAI cost per premium query monthly before increasing quotas.
